Privacy Policy for Bible Journey

Last Updated: June 26, 2026

Welcome to Bible Journey ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information when you use our services.

1. Information Collected by the App

Bible Journey is designed to respect user privacy. We minimize data collection to provide you with a secure, local, and premium study experience:

  • Local Study History: To support features like tracking your last 25 read verses and the Swap Navigation feature, the app temporarily saves your reading footprints. This data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Interactive Maps: Our verse-to-map location feature uses geographical data to show historical locations. We do NOT track your personal GPS or physical location.
  • Quiz Progress: Any score or progress made within the Bible Quiz feature is managed locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app may utilize third-party services (such as Google Play Services or mapping infrastructure) to render maps or deliver standard app updates. These third parties handle data in compliance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Data Security

Because your reading history, smart search queries, and quiz progress are stored locally on your device, your data is as secure as your physical device. We do not sell, rent, or distribute any user information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Children's Privacy

Bible Journey is a safe environment for readers of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon being posted.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us via our official developer contact channels listed on the Google Play Store.
